2 · How the course works

8 units, 24 chapters, each tagged with the Idaho standards (9-12.EC.1–24). The units are stops on a trail from the Trailhead to The Peak.

In every chapter a student:

  1. Reads the chapter.
  2. Passes the chapter quiz: 20 questions, 80% to pass, unlimited retries.
  3. Answers Think Like an Economist, a short-answer question.

After 3 chapters: the unit test (20 questions, 70% to pass, 2 tries, best score counts). Passing it clears the unit and raises the student's trail rank (Greenhorn → Summiteer).

XP: 25 per task · 5 per day logged in · 50 per approved side quest.

Everything is graded by the server. Answer keys never reach students' browsers.

3 · Students sign in

On the site's Log in button: class period + name + a 4-digit PIN they choose. No email needed. Forgot a PIN? Reset it on your roster.

4 · Your dashboard tabs

Roster & scoresPick a period. Summary cards, then each student's XP, rank, progress bar, best unit test scores (red under 70%), last active day, and PIN reset. Needs help flags students who've been inactive 7+ days, failed a unit test, or haven't passed any quizzes yet.
Answer keysEvery chapter quiz, unit test, and Think Like an Economist model answer.
Side questsReal-world projects students turn in. Read the note, then Approve (+50 XP).
Ask {{TEACHER}}Anonymous student questions. Answer, then Post to the class board, mark Answered in class, or Skip.
Daily BriefingA short announcement students see when they open the site, plus today's survey results.
SuggestionsStudent ideas and bug reports. Mark them done or archive them.

5 · Sample Class

20 fake students so you can explore before real students join. They can't log in and never count in survey or debate results. On the roster: Remove sample class when you're ready, or Reset to bring it back fresh for a demo.

First-week checklist
  • Take the tour, then open Chapter 1 and try its quiz yourself.
  • Give students the link and have them log in with their period number.
  • Write a welcome note in Daily Briefing.
  • Remove the Sample Class once your real roster shows up.
  • Check Side quests and Ask once or twice a week.
